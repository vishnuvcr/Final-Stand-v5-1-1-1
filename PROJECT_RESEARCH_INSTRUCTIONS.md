# Project Research Instructions

This file records the project's reproducibility and research-governance instructions.

- Maintain a detailed phase plan in RESEARCH_PLAN.md.
- Keep each research phase on a separate Git branch.
- Provide a manual GitHub Actions workflow for each phase.
- Update README.md, RESEARCH_LOG.md, and ERROR_LOG.md after meaningful steps and failures.
- Cache important research data and persist structured filtered data/results.
- Search broadly for relevant market data and literature; document source provenance.
- For market research, consider NSE/BSE data, option data, FII/DII, volatility/regime, global cross-market effects, corporate actions and news where applicable.
- Account for slippage, transaction costs and broker charges; keep assumptions explicit and date-aware.
- Do not expose hidden chain-of-thought. Archive concise, reproducible decisions, observations, errors and results instead.
- Stop at the defined research phases and produce the final structured manuscript.
