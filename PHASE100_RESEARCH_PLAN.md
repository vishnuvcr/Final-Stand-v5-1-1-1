## Research question
Across the fixed 14-paper corpus and the registered methods, which claims are independently reproduced, partially replicated, inconclusive, not reproduced, or data-blocked after common-data screening, source-specific audit, uncertainty controls, and executable-options data requirements?

## Aims
1. Consolidate phase outputs without turning a proxy/screen into an exact reproduction.
2. Maintain a paper/method-level status for every registered item; carry unresolved methods forward as explicit blockers.
3. Publish a structured manuscript with abstract, research questions, aims/objectives, literature review, methodology, statistical analysis, results, inferences, discussion, strengths/limitations, conclusion, future research, tables, figures, appendices and supplements.
4. Preserve the difference between predictive accuracy, analytical payoff verification, and costed executable strategy performance.
5. Record a finite stopping decision. No strategy is promoted unless all data, robustness, cost, and validation gates pass.

## Method
- Ingest the latest Phase 95 branch outputs and the accepted Phase 96–99 outputs.
- Verify workflow status and data provenance. A failed reconciliation or stale artifact is labelled accordingly.
- Synthesize Phase 95 metrics against persistence; Phase 96 SMA/EMA screen; Phase 97 ML options gate; Phase 98 CCI gate; Phase 99 payoff algebra and source ambiguities.
- Build paper-level statuses from the registered 14-paper corpus; never infer exact replication from common-data family screening.
- Publish machine-readable phase summary and source/method status table, Markdown manuscript, supplement, SVG figure(s), and validation report.
- Explicitly list all data blockers and negative/inconclusive results. No 2026 holdout access.

## Acceptance and stop
- All phase artifacts are traceable to their source branch/run.
- Claims match accepted outputs and state limitations.
- Every paper/method has a final state: REPRODUCED, PARTIAL, NOT_REPRODUCED, INCONCLUSIVE, DATA_BLOCKED, or NOT_IDENTIFIABLE.
- Manuscript and supplements pass automated completeness checks.
- Phase 100 is the finite stop. Further work requires a new research plan, not an unbounded extension of this program.
