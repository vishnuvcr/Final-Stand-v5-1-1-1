# Phase 50B-3 Literature Review — India VIX Conditioning

The literature supports testing volatility regimes as a hypothesis, but does not establish profitability of any Phase-50B strategy.

1. Recent NIFTY weekly-expiry research reports regime dependence in variance risk premia and finds that the High-VIX regime can have distinct realized-volatility and directional characteristics. The repository treats this as external hypothesis-generating evidence only.
2. Research on the India VIX/NIFTY relationship documents asymmetric responses and volatility-state effects, supporting entry-time regime stratification.
3. Indian options-market work finds variance risk premia and emphasizes that short-volatility returns can be strongly affected by tail risk and market microstructure.
4. Recent work on NIFTY volatility modelling reports predictive information in ATM and OTM/skew features, reinforcing the need to treat VIX as one conditioning variable rather than a standalone trading signal.
