# Phase 26 Manuscript Supplement — Direct OTM7/8/9 Structure

### Research question
Does fixed OTM7/8/9 (buy OTM7, sell OTM8 and OTM9) avoid historical losses relative to the locked dynamic-n construction?

### Methods
The corrected minute-level engine was run over the 190-trade executable universe. Stage-1 direction was held constant using the OTM6/7/8 chooser. Fixed n=7 changed only the three-leg construction. Entry was 10:00 IST at four trading sessions before expiry; target was 90% of the structure's own entry-time payoff expression. One adverse ₹0.05 tick per leg, six orders, ₹10/order brokerage, date-aware statutory charges and historical lot sizes were retained.

### Results
Fixed OTM7/8/9 produced 180 positive trades and 10 negative trades versus 179 positive and 11 negative trades for dynamic-n. It converted exactly one dynamic-n loss into a win: 2026-03-17, from −₹632.58 to +₹5,850.83. No dynamic-n winner became a fixed-789 loss. Aggregate net P&L nevertheless declined from ₹138,937.12 to ₹129,567.71 (−₹9,369.41).

### Conclusion
The direct corrected test does not support a two-loss saving claim. It supports one loss-to-win conversion, accompanied by a lower aggregate P&L. The result is descriptive historical evidence and does not establish future performance.
